I’m David, an AI/ML Engineer and full stack developer based in Cluj-Napoca, Romania. I’ve been working as an AI Engineer at RebelDot since November 2024, building AI applications for automotive, insurance and enterprise clients.

My main focus is RAG and LLM systems: helping models find useful information, produce clear answers and show the evidence behind them. I’ve built an email assistant that prepares replies for human review, a system for assessing call quality, and cloud AI applications. My work also covers backend APIs, databases and performance improvements.

I like taking ownership of the whole process, from understanding the problem and planning the solution to building, testing and delivering it. I use clear metrics to check what works and improve what doesn’t. I also care about making the work easy for others to follow, with shared coding practices and practical documentation.

When I’m not behind a computer, you can find me cycling, training for my next race, playing a match of tennis or DJing at private events. I enjoy having different things to focus on outside work and always having something to look forward to.
