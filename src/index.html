I’m David, an AI/ML Engineer and full stack developer based in Cluj-Napoca, Romania. I’ve been working as an AI Engineer at RebelDot since November 2024, building AI applications for automotive, insurance and enterprise clients.

My main focus is RAG and LLM systems: helping models find useful information, produce clear answers and show the evidence behind them. I’ve built an email assistant that prepares replies for human review, a system for assessing call quality, and cloud AI applications. My work also covers backend APIs, databases and performance improvements.

I like taking ownership of the whole process, from understanding the problem and planning the solution to building, testing and delivering it. I use clear metrics to check what works and improve what doesn’t. I also care about making the work easy for others to follow, with shared coding practices and practical documentation.

When I’m not behind a computer, you can find me cycling, training for my next race, playing a match of tennis or DJing at private events. I enjoy having different things to focus on outside work and always having something to look forward to.

Reported measurements for the integrated pipeline.

What I built

  • Retrieval with the right context. Routed requests across 11 categories, combined Cohere embeddings with PostgreSQL/pgvector search, and used Haiku reranking to prioritize evidence before drafting.
  • A reusable knowledge pipeline. Turned historical agent replies into source-grounded knowledge. The integrated pipeline raised reported correct drafting from 45% to 88% and top-5 retrieval from 70% to 91%.
  • Preparation before indexing. Added GLiNER/Presidio PII masking, MPNet semantic clustering and DeBERTa NLI grounding checks to limit personal-data exposure and keep answers traceable to their sources.
  • Repeatable prompt optimization. Replaced ad hoc tuning with DSPy/GEPA against defined classification and safety metrics, making prompt selection repeatable and quality changes measurable.

I built weighted, rubric-driven LLM scoring with evidence for each criterion. RapidFuzz and DeBERTa NLI flag fabricated citations and unsupported reasoning without extra LLM verification calls.

What I built

  • Scoring with inspectable evidence. Turned transcripts into weighted, rubric-driven assessments with citations for each criterion, making quality reviews consistent and scores auditable.
  • Verification without extra LLM calls. Combined RapidFuzz citation checks with DeBERTa NLI to flag fabricated citations and unsupported reasoning. Local NLI checks benchmarked at 108–151 ms per criterion; these are advisory checks, not a factuality guarantee or total application latency.
  • Optimization against human agreement. Used DSPy/GEPA with macro-F1 and Cohen’s kappa across eight criteria and 119 calls (952 judgments) to measure agreement and identify quality regressions.
