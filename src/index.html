The engineering behind it ＋

Retrieve the right evidence

Classification routes requests across 11 categories. Cohere embeddings and PostgreSQL/pgvector retrieve relevant knowledge; Haiku reranks it before drafting source-cited replies.

Make knowledge reusable

GLiNER and Presidio mask PII, MPNet clusters related replies, and DeBERTa NLI checks grounding before indexing. Source traceability keeps answers reviewable.

Optimize with a repeatable test

DSPy/GEPA replaces ad hoc prompt tuning with optimization against classification and safety metrics. The reported gains belong to the integrated pipeline, rather than one isolated tool.
