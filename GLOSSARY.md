Public descriptions of David’s engineering experience and future personal work.

## Language

**Professional experience**:
Work David has carried out in his role at RebelDot, described through approved anonymized summaries.
_Avoid_: Personal projects, selected delivery

**Proposed personal project**:
An independent project recommendation that has not yet been built and evaluated for this portfolio.
_Avoid_: Completed project, live demo

**Human-reviewed draft**:
An AI-generated email response intended for review by a person before sending.
_Avoid_: Automatically sent response

**Grounding flag**:
An advisory indication that a citation or reasoning may lack supporting evidence.
_Avoid_: Factuality guarantee

**Reported outcome**:
A measurement supplied by David for the stated system and benchmark scope; it is not an independently reproduced portfolio benchmark.
_Avoid_: Universal performance guarantee
