# Rendered reference audit

Audited 15 October 2026: [nicolaemogage.com](https://nicolaemogage.com/), in an actual browser at the default desktop viewport (approximately 1274 × 717) and a 390 × 844 mobile viewport. Desktop hero, experience, project grid and contact were visually inspected; mobile hero, expanded menu and contact were visually inspected. Accessibility snapshots supplied the full section structure. Both themes and section navigation were exercised. No form was submitted, and the reference's claims and message delivery were not independently verified.

## What works

- Strong type hierarchy: a wide geometric display sans gives the hero confidence, with a quieter serif for long-form explanation. The personal introduction and role immediately establish context.
- Dark ink/navy, warm ivory and burnt-orange accents feel restrained. The light theme switches to pale warm surfaces, navy headings and muted wine accents.
- A compact persistent header, active section underline and generous section spacing make a long page navigable. The mobile hamburger expands into full-width rows and closes after choosing a section.
- Hero actions make work, contact and the CV immediately available; recognizable social icons are separated by a thin rule.
- Experience demonstrates ownership through detailed implementation bullets. Project cards show year, category, description and technology tags; desktop uses two columns with generous card padding.
- A portrait, interests and personal writing supply humanity. Contact combines direct links with a form; on mobile it stacks links before the fields.

## What I would improve

- The experience section reads much like an expanded CV: very long cards, many bullets and extensive tool tags slow scanning. Lead with the challenge, personal responsibility and strongest evidence, then let readers expand technical detail.
- The dark hero's secondary headline and body copy appear subdued; David should use stronger text contrast and comfortable body sizing rather than copying that contrast treatment.
- Scroll/reveal transitions can briefly show large empty panels while content animates into place. Essential content should remain available without animation, and reduced-motion preferences should remove transitions.
- The mobile hero wraps gracefully but the long introduction consumes most of the screen before actions. David's opening can be shorter and more outcome-focused.
- Contact form availability is not evidence of delivery. David has no configured service requirement; explicit email/phone links are clearer and need less maintenance.
- A blog and a large project catalogue only belong where real material exists. David should launch with professional case studies and a clearly marked future roadmap rather than empty sections or proposed work presented as shipped.

## Original direction for David

Use the reference's confident hierarchy, generous spacing, useful navigation and deliberate detail. Create an original editorial identity around warm ivory, deep ink and a restrained copper or muted green accent; avoid copying its monogram, headline wording, portrait treatment or achievements.

The strongest content sequence is a concise AI/ML and full-stack introduction, two prominent anonymized engineering case studies, earlier platform work, grouped capabilities, credentials/education, a short running/triathlon note and direct contact. Treat the RAG email metrics as user-reported measurements and retain human review. Show call-quality evidence and advisory grounding limits. A small original CSS/SVG system-flow visual can communicate engineering thinking without confidential screenshots or a fabricated portrait.

Keep case studies readable: problem → ownership → engineering choices → evidence. Distinguish professional work from proposed personal projects. Technology names should explain an engineering choice, with searchable keywords retained. Keep navigation as semantic anchor links with visible focus, ensure mobile controls work with a keyboard, and use accessible native disclosure if more detail is needed.

## Evidence and limitations

Browser screenshots were captured and inspected in the audit tool for desktop hero, experience, contact, project cards, mobile hero/menu/contact, and the light-theme contact. They are transient inspection evidence rather than redistributed copies of the reference's assets. The browser viewport override was reset after mobile review. This is a visual/content audit, not a complete accessibility, security, performance or link audit of the reference.
