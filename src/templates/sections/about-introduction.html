About me.

I’m David, an AI/ML Engineer and full stack developer based in Cluj-Napoca, Romania, with four years of professional software engineering experience. I’ve been working as an AI Engineer at RebelDot since November 2024, building AI applications for automotive, insurance and enterprise clients. Before that, I spent 2022–2024 as an iOS developer at CHR Mobile Solutions, building native apps in Swift and UIKit.

My main focus is RAG and LLM systems: helping models find useful information, produce clear answers and show the evidence behind them. I designed the architecture of an email assistant that prepares replies for human review, from the Python pipeline and FastAPI backend to the Next.js and TypeScript review app. I’ve also built a system for assessing call quality and cloud AI applications, and my work covers backend APIs, databases and performance improvements.

I like taking ownership of the whole process, from understanding the problem and planning the solution to building, testing and delivering it. I use clear metrics to check what works and improve what doesn’t. I also care about making the work easy for others to follow, with shared coding practices and practical documentation.

When I’m not behind a computer, you can find me cycling, training for my next race, playing a match of tennis or DJing at private events. I enjoy having different things to focus on outside work and always having something to look forward to.
