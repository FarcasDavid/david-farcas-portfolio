What I built

  • Scoring with inspectable evidence. Turned transcripts into weighted, rubric-driven assessments with citations for each criterion, making quality reviews consistent and scores auditable.
  • Verification without extra LLM calls. Combined RapidFuzz citation checks with DeBERTa NLI to flag fabricated citations and unsupported reasoning. Local NLI checks benchmarked at 108–151 ms per criterion; these are advisory checks, not a factuality guarantee or total application latency.
  • Optimization against human agreement. Used DSPy/GEPA with macro-F1 and Cohen’s kappa across eight criteria and 119 calls (952 judgments) to measure agreement and identify quality regressions.
